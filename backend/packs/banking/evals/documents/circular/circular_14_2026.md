# Circular 14/2026: Dormant account reactivation (SAMPLE)

## Purpose

This circular changes how dormant accounts are reactivated, to reduce the time customers wait.

## Changes

- Tellers may now reactivate a dormant account at any branch, not only the home branch
- The customer completes the reactivation form and shows a valid photo ID
- Branch manager approval is required only for accounts dormant for more than five years

## Effective date

The changes take effect on 1 November 2026.
