# KYC Policy (SAMPLE)

## 1 Purpose

This policy sets out how staff identify and verify customers before opening an account or
carrying out a transaction.

## 4.1 Individual accounts

The customer must provide one valid government-issued photo ID (Ghana Card or passport) and
proof of address dated within the last three months.

## 4.2 Joint accounts

Each holder of a joint account must provide their own valid government-issued photo ID (Ghana
Card or passport) and proof of address dated within the last three months. All holders must
sign the joint mandate form in the presence of a member of staff.

## 4.3 Minors

An account for a customer under 18 is opened by a parent or guardian, who provides their own ID
and the minor's birth certificate.
