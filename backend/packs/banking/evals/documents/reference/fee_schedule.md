# Fee schedule (SAMPLE)

## F01 Monthly account maintenance

GHS 10.00 a month on current accounts, taken on the last working day of the month. Waived for
the month when the average balance is at least GHS 5,000.00. Savings accounts pay no
maintenance fee.

## F07 SMS alerts

GHS 2.50 a month for transaction alerts by SMS, taken on the last working day of the month. The
customer can switch alerts off at a branch or in the mobile app; the fee stops the next month.

## F12 Returned cheque

GHS 50.00 for each cheque the bank returns unpaid because the drawer's account could not pay
it, taken from the drawer's account the next working day. Not charged when the cheque is
returned for a technical reason, such as a signature that differs (R02).

## F15 Statement reprint

GHS 5.00 a page for a statement printed at a branch. Statements in the mobile app are free.
