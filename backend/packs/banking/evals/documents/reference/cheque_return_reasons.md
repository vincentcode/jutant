# Cheque return reasons (SAMPLE)

The clearing house's codes for a cheque returned unpaid, and what to tell the customer.

## R01 Insufficient funds

The drawer's account did not have enough available balance when the cheque was presented.
Funds on hold (a lien, uncleared deposits) do not count. The returned cheque fee (F12) is
charged to the drawer. Next step: the payee may present the cheque again once the drawer has
funded the account.

## R02 Signature differs

The signature does not match the mandate on file. No fee is charged. Next step: the drawer
should issue a new cheque, or update the specimen signature at the branch.

## R03 Stale cheque

The cheque was presented more than six months after its date. Next step: the drawer must issue
a new cheque.

## R05 Payment stopped by drawer

The drawer asked the bank to stop the cheque before it was presented. Next step: the payee
should contact the drawer.
