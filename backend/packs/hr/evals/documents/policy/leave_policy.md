# Leave Policy (SAMPLE)

## 1 Purpose

This policy sets out the kinds of leave employees may take, how much, and how to ask for it.

## 2 Annual leave

Permanent and fixed-term employees have 21 working days of annual leave a year. Leave is asked
for in the HR Assistant or the HR portal at least 3 working days ahead, and approved by the
line manager. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the first quarter of the next year; days
not taken by 31 March are lost.

## 3 Sick leave

Employees have 10 working days of paid sick leave a year. Sick leave may be recorded after the
absence. A doctor's note is needed for an absence of more than 2 working days in a row.

## 4 Compassionate leave

Employees may take up to 5 working days of compassionate leave a year on the death or serious
illness of a spouse, child, parent or sibling.

## 5 Maternity and paternity leave

Mothers have 12 weeks of paid maternity leave, asked for at least 30 days ahead with a medical
certificate of the due date. Fathers have 5 working days of paternity leave within one month of
the birth, with the child's birth certificate.

## 6 Study leave

Employees on an approved course have up to 5 working days of study leave a year for exams,
asked for at least 14 days ahead with the exam timetable.

## 7 Public holidays

Public holidays are not counted as leave. A public holiday that falls within approved leave is
given back.
