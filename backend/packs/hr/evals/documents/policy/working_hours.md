# Working Hours and Overtime Policy (SAMPLE)

## 1 Working hours

Normal working hours are 8:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday, with one hour for lunch.

## 2 Overtime

Overtime must be approved in advance by the line manager. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times
the hourly rate on weekdays and 2 times on weekends and public holidays, in the next month's pay.
Instead of payment, an employee may agree with their manager to take time off in lieu within
three months.

## 3 Working from home

With their manager's agreement, employees whose role allows it may work from home up to 2 days a
week. Branch and customer-facing roles are not eligible.
